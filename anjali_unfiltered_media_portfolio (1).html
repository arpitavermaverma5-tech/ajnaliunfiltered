01 / Introduction

Curiosity is where every story begins.

A little about the person behind the portfolio.

I'm Anjali, currently pursuing a Bachelor of Journalism and Mass Communication (BJMC) at K.R. Mangalam University. I'm passionate about the power of media to inform, connect and inspire people.

From digital journalism and anchoring to podcasting and video creation, I'm excited by the many ways stories can reach an audience. I enjoy meeting people, exchanging ideas and learning something new from every experience.

I'm building my skills step by step, with an open mind, a creative spirit and a genuine interest in the world around me.
